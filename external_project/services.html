Services

Hydraulics & Suspensions

Hydraulics and suspensions require the removal of all the factory suspension from the car and replacing it with hydraulic rams to allow the car to lift and lower at the flip of a switch. Battery trays are then made and installed in the trunk to power the hydraulic pumps that provide the oil and pressure to raise the car. Once installed, the hydraulic hoses are fed from the pumps inside the trunk to each cylinder on all four corners of the car. Lastly, all electrical components are wired to make the car function properly.

Frame Reinforcement

Frame reinforcing is when templates are made of the car's frame and transferred onto metal. Metal shapes are then cut and welded onto the car's frame so it can hold up to the power of the hydraulic system. The more power that is added to the hydraulic system, the more reinforcing that must be done.

Metal Work

Metal work is done when a car or truck has rusted body panels. The rusted areas are then cut out and replaced with new metal. This is also done by welding the new cut-out pieces into the area that is being replaced.

Basic Fabrication

Basic fabrication is “cosmetic” work being done. This may be achieved by adding a little more flash to the hydraulic system to make for a better appearance. The hydraulic hoses are usually replaced with hand-bent stainless-steel lines, which allow for a non-clutter appearance.

Custom Fabrication

Custom Fabrication is when a pedestal is created for the hydraulic pump mounts, making them appear to float. In this process, a more in-depth installation of the hydraulic system in the trunk is done to make it more appealing to the eye. This includes painting or chroming parts of the system to match the paint of the car.
